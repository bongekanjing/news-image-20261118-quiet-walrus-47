APEC · SHENZHEN

Xi and Putin open summit on the eve of a first three-way meeting with Trump

The Chinese and Russian presidents met in Shenzhen on Wednesday before Thursday's trilateral. Xi urged support for the WTO and free trade; Putin called sanctions “a thing of the past”. Trump was in Manila for ASEAN and the Quad, and said he hoped to meet Kim Jong Un in China.

GULF · IRAN

Iranian drones hit US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain; US jets strike Qeshm

Iran's army said it targeted Patriot batteries at Ali Al Salem; sirens sounded in Bahrain for a third time in five days. US warplanes hit an IRGC missile site on Qeshm Island. Two tankers burned in the Strait of Hormuz after mines, and Qatar opened new talks with Tehran.

ISRAEL · KNESSET

Eisenkot's ally takes the speaker's chair, 66 to 52

Matan Kahana beat Likud's candidate after a seven-hour debate. Neither bloc can reach 61 seats, so the 19 Arab-led and 13 Haredi seats now decide who governs; Eisenkot has until 6 December to form a government.
